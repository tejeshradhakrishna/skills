# AI and Economics Coverage

Activate AI coverage only for models, agents, autonomous actions, or probabilistic outputs. Activate economics when cost, incentives, pricing, vendor exposure, or resource competition can change the decision.

## AI and automation

- Model or agent authority, tool permissions, human approval, override, and containment
- Accuracy, calibration, uncertainty, abstention, and harmful failure behavior
- Evaluation datasets, adversarial tests, drift detection, and monitoring
- Prompt injection, data exfiltration, unsafe actions, and least-privilege boundaries
- Provenance, explainability, disclosure, audit trail, and failure ownership
- Provider portability, fallback, latency, cost, and data handling

## Economics and incentives

- Who pays, benefits, bears risk, can block, and maintains the outcome
- Unit economics, cost growth, pricing assumptions, and resource ceilings
- Incentives for misuse, gaming, metric manipulation, or risk transfer
- Switching cost, vendor leverage, licensing, and long-term operating burden

## Probes

- Give the model ambiguous, adversarial, or out-of-distribution input.
- Remove the preferred provider or human approver.
- Test silent quality drift and unavailable evaluation data.
- Let a rational actor optimize the metric against the real objective.
- Scale usage or model cost by 10×.
- Ask who is accountable when a probabilistic action causes harm.
- Identify which decision changes if the weakest economic assumption is false.

Interrupt when automation can act beyond verified authority, evaluation cannot support the claimed behavior, fallback repeats the same failure mode, or incentives reward unsafe or misleading outcomes.
