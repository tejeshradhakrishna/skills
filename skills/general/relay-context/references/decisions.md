# Decisions and Continuation Risks

Include only information that can change what the next agent should do.

Capture settled decisions with rationale, consequence, and owner or date only when known. Distinguish approved decisions from proposals and assumptions. Preserve user intent even when implementation evidence differs.

Record:

- Governing constraints and non-negotiables.
- Rejected approaches or failed attempts whose repetition would waste effort.
- Dependencies and their effect on ordering.
- Active blockers and risks, including impact and mitigation when known.
- Open questions that require the user rather than unilateral agent choice.
- Deferred work that is intentionally outside the next outcome.

Rank next actions by dependency and value. Give the first action an exact resume point plus its verification or stop condition.

Do not turn ordinary discussion into permanent decisions. Omit rationale that does not affect continuation, and do not invent owners, deadlines, approvals, or risk severity.
